What I Did During University

  • I received my MSc and BSc in mathematics from the University of Toronto in 2023 and 2022 respectively.
  • I worked with Masoud Khalkhali and Asghar Ghorbanpour researching trace formulas for graphs as part of the the Fields Undergraduate Research Summer Program in the summer of 2021. Read more on the Axioms journal about what we did.
  • Community Involvement at University:
    • I was a math department Steward for our labour union, CUPE 3902 Unit 1.
    • I was president of UofT's Student Chapter of the Association for Women in Mathematics (AWM) for the 2020-2021 school year; follow AWM on Instagram.
    • I was president of UofT's undergraduate Math Union (MU) for the 2021-2022 school year; follow MU on instagram!
  • Teaching Experience:
    • I was a Teaching Assistant for MAT223: Linear Algebra I for the 2023 Summer semester.
    • I was a Teaching Assistant for MAT240: Algebra I for the 2022 Fall semester.
    • I was a tutorial Teaching Assistant for MAT187: Engineering Calculus II for the Winter semesters for 2021 - 2023.
    • I was a tutorial Teaching Assistant for MAT188: Engineering Linear Algebra for the 2021 Fall semester.
